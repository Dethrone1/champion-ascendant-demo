What this list is

A shortlist, not a census. It's drawn from fighters already on the ONE and DWCS boards. A fighter who was never added to either board can't appear here. Fighters inactive for more than 12 months are left off.

Conservative inputs. Where a title win couldn't be confirmed in fight records, it was scored as zero. Flagged fighters have a record detail still being checked; the flag says what.

ONE ASCENT v3.9 is in validation. It benchmarks a fighter against ONE Championship's MMA signings since 2023. DWCS ASCENT v3.0 benchmarks against Contender Series invitees. Neither predicts who gets signed, who wins, or who gets a contract.

Known bias. Both formulas give LFA heavy credit, because LFA genuinely feeds both promotions. Five of the eleven names fight in LFA, and part of that overlap comes from the formulas, not only the fighters.
