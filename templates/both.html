What this is. A shortlist from the fighters already on the ONE and DWCS boards, not a census of every unsigned fighter. A fighter who was never added to either board cannot appear here.

  • ONE ASCENT v3.8.1 is in validation. It benchmarks a fighter against ONE Championship signings since 2023. The retired ONE board ran v3.7.1.
  • DWCS ASCENT v3.0 benchmarks a fighter against the measured profile of Contender Series invitees. It does not predict who wins on fight night or who gets a contract. It replaced v2.8.5 on 24 Oct 2026 after scoring better against actual invite outcomes (AUC 0.686 vs 0.628).
  • Known bias. Both formulas give LFA heavy credit, because LFA genuinely feeds both promotions. Six of the twelve names are LFA fighters, and part of that overlap comes from the formulas, not only the fighters.
  • Just missed: Laura Vasconcelos (ONE 90.7, DWCS 93.3, but inactive since May 2025; booked for LFA 246), Ieuan Davies (ONE 83.2), Murad Guseinov (ONE 79.5).
