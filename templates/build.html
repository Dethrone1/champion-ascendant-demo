Tested against the right target

The UFC score is meant to describe who gets invited to the Contender Series. Tested against invites, win streak turned out worse than a coin flip, while finishing was the strongest signal. Rebuilding around that improved the whole formula.

AUC: how well a score ranks invited fighters above the rest. 0.5 is a coin flip, 1.0 is perfect.
