Who gets signed, measured.

How it's built

Score a fighter

Enter a pro record. You get the ONE ASCENT and DWCS ASCENT scores separately, each broken into its parts. They're different formulas built on different data, so the two numbers aren't the same unit and shouldn't be compared.

Record
Finishes are wins by KO, TKO or submission.
Fighter
Main promotion (most recent fight)
Each formula weighs promotions by its own data, so the lists differ. Win streak and the DWCS promotion only affect DWCS; country only affects ONE.

What goes into each score

Every part traces back to something measured in the signing or invite data. The method page covers where each weight came from, what was tested, and what broke.

ONE ASCENT v3.9

PartPointsWhat it measures
Finish rateup to 65

Finishes per win, pulled toward the division average for short records, then scaled against that division's global finish rate. The biggest single part.

Win rateup to 15

Wins over all fights, draws included.

Promotion0 to 12

The promotion's share of ONE's MMA signings since 2023. ONE Friday Fights and LFA sit at the top.

Promotion region0 to 2

Share of signings by the promotion's home region.

Origin region2 to 6

Share of signings by where the fighter is from.

Age−4 to +3

23 and under gets the most. 34 and over loses 4. Not a claim about ability.

Title wins3 + 1 each

Pro titles only, uncapped. None scores nothing.

Undefeated+5

No losses and at least five wins.

DWCS ASCENT v3.0

PartPointsWhat it measures
Finish rateup to 45

Scaled against the division average, capped at 1.55× so the top of the board still ranks.

Win rateup to 6

Cut from 15 in v3.0. Every invitee wins, so it barely separates them.

Loss band0 to 8

Zero and one loss look almost the same among invitees. Two losses drops off sharply.

Win streaksquare root

Scores below chance against invites (AUC 0.485), so its weight was not raised.

Promotion3 to 15

Share of Season 9 invitees. LFA alone produced about a fifth.

Age−3 to +3

26 to 29 scores highest. Kept flat because two age signals disagreed.

Title winsuncapped

Scaled by promotion tier, with diminishing returns per belt.

Bonuses+4, +3

Elite finish rate, and a win streak of seven or more.

The DWCS total is rescaled so the invitee median sits at 78 and the 97th percentile at 100. That changes the units, not the order.

Champion Ascendant

A benchmark, not a prediction. ASCENT describes how closely a fighter matches the record profile of real signings and invites. It doesn't say who will get signed.
