Who gets signed, measured.

Champion Ascendant scores unsigned MMA fighters against the fighters who actually got signed. One score for ONE Championship, one for the UFC. The higher the score, the more likely a fighter is to get signed.

Score a fighter See Top Prospects

What does Champion Ascendant do?

Scores any fighter

Enter a pro record and get two scores: one for ONE Championship, one for the UFC.

Finds Top Prospects

A shortlist of unsigned fighters who score 85 or higher for both promotions.

Shows why

Every score breaks down into finish rate, record, promotion, profile and titles.

What each score looks at

ONE ASCENT

Built from the fighters ONE Championship has signed since 2023.

  • Finish rate
  • Record
  • Promotion
  • Age and origin
  • Titles

DWCS/UFC ASCENT

Built from the fighters the UFC brings in through the Contender Series. High scores also fit the fighters it signs directly.

  • Finish rate
  • Record and momentum
  • Promotion
  • Age
  • Titles

Champion Ascendant

Scores unsigned fighters on how closely they match the fighters who got signed.
