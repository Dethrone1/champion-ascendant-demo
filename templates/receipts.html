Three more (Bertolso, Moreno, Bulaid) were on the board first and announced afterwards, but the board was not public at that point, so there is no third-party record showing they were listed before the booking. That's a calibration result. It says the formula describes an invitee field consistently year to year. It says nothing about picking who gets the call.

THE OPEN WINDOW

Season 10 isn't fully booked. Eleven fighters on the roster still have no episode attached and a run of slots hasn't been announced at all. So the DWCS board as it stands on 11 August 2026 is frozen and dated against the part of the season nobody has seen yet.

If a fighter from that frozen board turns up in a booking announced after today, it goes here as a clean call with both dates shown. If none of them do, that goes here too, in the same size type. Season 11 gets the proper version of this: board frozen and published before any casting news, denominator fixed in advance, then count what happens.

HOW TO READ THIS PAGE

This isn't an accuracy claim. Clean prospective calls on this page: zero. Every Season 10 fighter here was already publicly booked when I scored him, which makes this a tracking log rather than a track record.

What it does give you is the fighters the board was carrying, the score they had, the version that produced it, and where they ended up, each entry dated and linked to primary records.

Season 10 entries stay frozen at v2.7.1. Re-scoring them once results are in would wreck the only thing that makes them worth reading. When board fighters stall out or lose, that goes on here the same way.
