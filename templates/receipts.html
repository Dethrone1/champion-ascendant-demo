What it shows

Six of nine left with contracts. All four fighters scored 85 or higher got one; below 85 it was two of five. Across the whole Season 10 field the frozen v2.7.1 column averaged 70.9, against 72.0 for Season 9, on a season the weights weren't built from. That's a calibration result: the formula describes an invitee field consistently from year to year.

What it doesn't show

Nine fighters is far too few to mean anything, and the DWCS score targets getting invited, not winning a contract. Most of these fighters were already booked and public when they were scored, so they are tracking, not calls. Bertolso, Moreno and Bulaid were on the board before their bookings were announced, but the board wasn't public then, so there's no third-party record. Three of the scores are v2.8.5 because those fighters weren't in the frozen v2.7.1 set.
